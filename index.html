Section Two

The Realtor inquiry is worth noting separately

One of the two showings came from a licensed agent working with a renter. That matters for a reason that isn't obvious.

Agents pre-screen. They have already had the budget conversation with their client, already narrowed the search, and do not spend time touring homes their renter cannot afford or will not want. When an agent books a showing, the prospect has usually been qualified before they ever reach the door.

It is also a signal about the listing itself. Your home is on the MLS, and agents searching that system are filtering the same way renters do. An agent finding it now, and not in the first two weeks, is consistent with everything above.

Section Three

What we are doing next

  • Following up with both interested parties directly after their showings — same-day where possible. This is where a good showing turns into an application, and it is the step most often skipped.
  • Debriefing the Realtor specifically. Agents will tell us candidly what their client thought, including objections a renter would never say to a listing agent's face. That feedback is worth more than any other we get.
  • Re-contacting the earlier inquiry from the first two weeks. That prospect passed at $3,150 and now has a concrete reason to look again.
  • Continuing to monitor 9305 Luminary Boulevard for any price movement so we respond before we are undercut rather than after.
  • Re-verifying the listing on every portal, logged out, as part of the weekly cycle.

Section Six

Our recommendation

Hold at $2,995

No change this week, and no decision needed from you.

The purpose of these reports is to tell you whether the price and the marketing are producing interest, and to recommend an adjustment if they are not. This week the answer is that they are. Moving the number again now would be reacting to the wrong thing.

What we want to see over the next seven days is whether these showings convert — either into an application, or into feedback specific enough to act on. Two showings is movement. It is not yet a pool of qualified applicants to choose from, and we will keep working toward that.

We also want to set a fair expectation. A showing is not a lease, and either of these prospects may pass. If activity stops after this week, we will tell you plainly and bring you a recommendation. If it continues, we hold and let the price keep doing its work.

You made a decision quickly last week when we asked you to, and it worked. We would rather tell you that plainly than pretend the improvement came from anything we did on the marketing side.
